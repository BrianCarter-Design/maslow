02 — User Flows
Key Journeys
The four most important flows on the platform.
🎯 Rate a Friend's Personality
1
Go to friend's profile
Or accept a "rate me" request notification
2
Choose test to rate them on
Enneagram, 16 Personalities, Big Five — same questions, different framing: "Jamie tends to…"
3
Complete the assessment
27 statements, same flow — phrased about the other person
4
Submit rating
Friend is notified. They can see your rating vs. their self-rating
→ Goes into their "How others see me" tab
5
Comparison unlocked
Both can now compare self-score vs. peer-score side by side
⚡ Compare Results with a Friend
1
Open Compare page
Or click "Compare" on any friend's profile
2
Select a friend
Search or pick from your connections list
3
Choose test type
Enneagram, 16 Personalities, Big Five — or "All" for a combined view
→ Only shows tests both have completed
4
View comparison
Side-by-side scores, radar chart overlay, compatibility score
5
Share or save
Post to feed, export as image, or invite another friend to join
🚀 New User Onboarding
1
Sign up
Email / Google / Apple — first name, profile photo
2
Take first assessment
Enneagram is the default entry point (27q, 5 min)
3
See your results
Full results page with type, wings, traits
4
Find friends
Import contacts, search by name, or share invite link
→ Suggested by compatible type
5
Land on Feed
See friends' activity, get prompted to rate a connection
🙋 Request Others to Rate You
1
Go to My Profile → "How others see me"
Shows a gap if no peer ratings yet
2
Tap "Request a Rating"
Choose which test + which friends to ask
3
Friends get a notification
"Jamie wants to know how you see them — take 5 min?"
4
Results come in
Aggregated anonymously if 3+ raters, or named if only 1–2
→ Notified when each rating is submitted
5
See perception gap
Self vs. others radar chart, type match %, blind spots callout
